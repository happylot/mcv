Service architecture

MCV packages six service groups into one cross-border growth operating system.

Each group solves a specific layer of the market-entry journey, from digital presence and lead management to merchant setup, compliance, retail access, and B2B trade.

6

6 execution modules

Businesses can start with one urgent module or roll out a synchronized 90-day roadmap.

01

SEO-ready corporate website

Build a conversion-oriented website for cross-border commerce with stronger messaging, category clarity, and lead capture.

  • Home page, service page, and category landing-page architecture
  • Optimized headings, internal links, semantic content, and CTAs
  • Buyer-facing content designed for the US and Southeast Asia
02

Omnichannel CRM integrated with zenify.cx

Bring leads, conversations, sales pipelines, and customer care into one operating center.

  • Unify leads from web, social, ads, inbox, telesales, and referrals
  • Design pipelines for sales, account, and customer success teams
  • Automate follow-up, task reminders, and conversion reporting
03

Dedicated payment gateway access

Structure merchant accounts and payment flows for each business model, including standard goods, complex categories, digital products, and services.

  • Merchant flows for consumer goods, wellness, food, and sensitive categories
  • Support for one-time payments, installments, subscriptions, or digital delivery
  • Guidance on acceptance rate, risk flow, and checkout experience
04

Legal services and company structuring

Help businesses complete the legal foundation required to operate cross-border commerce more safely.

  • Company formation, legal entity structuring, and operating documentation
  • Commercial structure guidance for the target market
  • Documentation for merchant onboarding and retail engagement
05

Certifications and compliance documentation

Support businesses while they complete the standards required to expand into more demanding markets.

  • ISO, FDA, Halal, GACC, and product-specific documentation sets
  • Compliance gap review before opening new sales channels
  • Turn certifications into sales assets, not just archived paperwork
06

Trade promotion into major retail chains

Prepare businesses to approach Walmart, Costco, and larger retail or distribution networks.

  • Build retail pitch decks, line sheets, margin stories, and commercial profiles
  • Prepare buyer-ready materials for early-stage meetings
  • Coordinate with promotion partners to open more B2B opportunities in the US and Southeast Asia
Service combinations

MCV typically deploys around three practical service combinations.

Launch

For businesses just opening a cross-border channel: SEO website, Zenify CRM, and merchant foundation.

Compliance Lift

For businesses with customers already, but blocked by legal setup, certifications, or payment acceptance rate.

Retail Entry

For businesses entering retail chains or expanding B2B with more professional buyer-facing assets.
