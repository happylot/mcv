About MCV

MCV was built to become the commercial operating layer that helps Vietnamese businesses enter international markets with structure.

Instead of hiring separate vendors for websites, CRM, merchant accounts, legal work, and trade promotion, businesses can work with one lead partner capable of designing the full operating system for cross-border e-commerce.

MCV GLOBAL JSC Hanoi, Vietnam US + Southeast Asia focus
OS

MCV positioning

A company providing a full operating system for businesses building cross-border e-commerce, connecting growth infrastructure with compliance readiness and market access.

  • Growth infrastructure
  • Merchant and payment enablement
  • Compliance and retail expansion
Vision

To become the trusted cross-border commerce operations partner for Vietnamese businesses expanding internationally.

MCV aims to turn international expansion from a fragmented set of tasks into an operating system that can be repeated and scaled.

Mission

Design digital infrastructure, payments, legal setup, and commercial enablement so businesses can enter the US and Southeast Asia with less friction.

From SEO-ready websites to compliance documents, every implementation layer is meant to shorten time to market.

Operating principles

MCV operates around three core principles.

01

One execution lead

Clients should not have to stitch together multiple small vendors for problems that are tightly connected.

02

Designed for the target market

The US and Southeast Asia have different growth, payment, and compliance logic; MCV does not use one template everywhere.

03

Turn compliance into growth

Legal setup, certifications, and documentation are not only for eligibility. They also increase buyer trust during the sales process.

How MCV works

MCV combines three capability layers into one complete journey.

A

Growth infrastructure

SEO-ready websites, buyer-facing content, omnichannel CRM, and automation to generate and nurture leads.

B

Merchant and compliance

Payment gateways, legal setup, ISO, FDA, Halal, GACC, and the documentation layers required for each product group.

C

Market expansion

Trade promotion, retail outreach, buyer-ready documentation, and sales coordination to enter larger partner networks.

"Businesses do not need another isolated service provider. They need a machine for market entry."

That is why MCV is shaped as an operating system, not an agency focused only on marketing or a vendor focused only on legal work.

Market focus

Focused on businesses opening access to the US and Southeast Asia.

MCV fits manufacturers, brand owners, distributors, and service companies that need to restructure their sales infrastructure to operate cross-border more effectively.
